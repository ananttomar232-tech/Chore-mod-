## What was changed (CC-BY asks you to indicate changes)

| Model | Changes |
|---|---|
| Hacker's room | Cut down to the L-shaped desk setup (walls, sofas, arcade cabinet removed), retro beige restyled to black gunmetal, posters & screens that showed third-party brands (film poster, game logos, magazine covers) **replaced with original art** (Black Market poster, Zero Day, Wanted: Unicorn, Heist Plan, Limitless OS, breach terminal), screens made emissive. |
| Briefcase | Re-coloured to carbon-black leather with gold locks, scaled. |
| Mona Lisa | Scaled to 2.6 blocks, turned into a wall-mounted block. (The painting itself is public domain.) |
| Freeze ray | Scaled, used as a held item. |
| Unicorn | Restyled "dark & dangerous": obsidian coat, black/crimson mane, blade-like sharpened wing feathers, spiked mane/tail locks, glowing red eyes, horn turned into an animated **dark-rainbow emissive** horn; decimated to 17k triangles and rigged (8 bones) for procedural animation. |
| CC-AR2 rifle | Decimated to 6k triangles, scaled. |
| Weapon rack | The military emblem and lettering (a video-game trademark) **removed**, original "Weapon Cartel" crest painted in its place, emissive strips added. |
| Japanese neon signs | All 18 signs **re-drawn with English translations** (same colours / shapes); 16 extra original designs added. |

Everything else (VIP golem suit, sunglasses, lava suit, mystery box, dragon core, grappling gun, crazy apple, icons, all
code) is original work made for this mod.

Fonts used to paint the neon / poster textures: **Inter** and **JetBrains Mono** (SIL Open Font License).
